## Status and streak
- Success is blue rather than green, preventing confusion with the core brand color. Always pair status color with an icon or label.
- Keep the streak indicator orange: `#C96F18` in light mode and `#F1A24A` in dark mode.

## Logo rules
- Clear space: one seed width on every side.
- Minimum digital sizes: symbol 24 px; app icon 29 px; horizontal lockup 96 px wide; stacked lockup 72 px wide.
- Never close the ring openings, rotate the symbol, add a checkmark/brain/flame, or use neon/cyan effects.
