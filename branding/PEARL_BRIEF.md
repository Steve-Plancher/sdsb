# Brand brief: SDSB — Steve Digital Second Brain

You are helping me create the full brand identity for my app, SDSB. Use Canva and
your image-generation skills. Work in two stages: first show me 3 distinct concepts
and let me pick one, then build out the full system from the concept I choose. Don't
finalize anything before I've picked.

## The app

SDSB ("Steve Digital Second Brain") is a daily todo list and habit tracker with
charts: streaks, a 30-day trend line, and a calendar-style consistency heatmap. It
is being built as a native iOS app, with a web version too. The app has three tabs:
**Today**, **Habits**, **Insights**. It is not a note-taking app, despite the name.

It's a personal tool for me right now, but the brand must be good enough to launch
on the App Store later without a redo.

## Brand direction (my decisions)

- **Feel:** calm and focused. Quiet, low-stress, a clear place to think. Plenty of
  space and soft contrast. Not loud, not gamified.
- **Independent identity:** no link to any other brand or theme I use. Don't reuse
  blue/cyan HUD or sci-fi styling.
- **Primary color:** green / teal. Aim for a calm, slightly muted green or teal, not
  neon or lime. It should suggest growth and health.
- **Logo:** an abstract symbol, with "SDSB" set beside it in the lockup.
- **Symbol meaning:** growth, for example a sprout, a leaf, or growth rings. It should
  suggest small daily habits compounding over time. Avoid clichés: no literal brains,
  lightbulbs, or plain checkmarks.
- **Name usage:** "SDSB" is the main name everywhere. Spell out "Steve Digital
  Second Brain" only on the splash screen, the About page and the App Store subtitle.
- **Modes:** design light and dark mode equally, and check both palettes fully.
  Don't make one an automatic inversion of the other.
- **Type:** a clean geometric sans. It must be free to embed in an app, under the SIL
  Open Font License. Good candidates are Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit or Sora.
  Recommend one and explain why.

## Deliverables

### 1. Logo and iOS app icon
- Primary symbol, plus horizontal and stacked lockups with "SDSB".
- App icon master at **1024×1024 px**, square with no transparency and no rounded
  corners (iOS applies the mask).
- iOS **light, dark and tinted** icon variants. Tinted means a single-color
  grayscale version that iOS recolors.
- The icon must still read clearly at **60 px and 29 px**. Keep it simple, with
  no fine lines and no text in the icon itself.
- A monochrome version of the symbol, one-color on light and on dark.

### 2. Color palette and type
- Primary brand color, one secondary or accent color, and neutrals. Give a **hex code
  for every color in both light and dark mode**.
- A **5-step single-hue ramp** of the primary color, from light to dark, for the
  consistency heatmap and progress rings.
- Status colors for success, warning and error, kept visually separate from the brand
  green so success doesn't blur into the brand color.
- A color for the **streak indicator**. It is currently an orange flame, so tell me
  whether it should stay orange or move closer to the palette.
- Contrast: body text must meet **WCAG AA, 4.5:1** against its background in both
  modes. Green text on white often fails, so give me a darker green for text if needed.
  List the text/background pairs you checked.
- Type scale: sizes and weights for the large title, title, headline, body, caption
  and the numbers used in stats.

### 3. Canva Brand Kit
- Load the logo, all colors (named clearly, for example "SDSB Green 500") and fonts
  into a **Canva Brand Kit named "SDSB"**, so future designs stay on-brand.

### 4. Launch assets
- A splash screen (light and dark) with the symbol and "Steve Digital Second Brain".
- An App Store screenshot template for iPhone at 1290×2796 px, with a headline area
  and a device frame.
- A social / profile image with the symbol on a brand background.

## How to present it

For each of the 3 concepts: the symbol, a one-line idea behind it, the palette, and a
mock of the app icon on an iPhone home screen in light and dark. After I pick, deliver
the full set above plus a one-page brand guide covering do's and don'ts, clear space
around the logo, and minimum sizes.
